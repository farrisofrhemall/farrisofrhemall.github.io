Week 01

Dopamine Mapping

🎯 Focus: Breaking Procrastination Loops

Stop waiting for "motivation" that never comes. Learn how to track your personal neurological spikes and design tiny novelty triggers that make starting boring tasks easy.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Rosemary
The Benefit: Rich in 1,8-cineole, proven to increase memory speed and focus.
Protocol: Steep 1 tbsp fresh leaves covered for 10 mins. Sip during your morning planning hour.

🧠 Understanding Procrastination Loops & Brain Overload

Why This Module Benefits You: Traditional planning demands linear focus, which starves an interest-driven nervous system. Dopamine mapping reveals when your brain is naturally primed to move.

Why Overload Happens: ADHD brains experience delayed or insufficient baseline dopamine synthesis. Faced with a multi-step task, your prefrontal cortex struggles to compute a "reward value." It views the assignment as an unrewarding monolith, flags it as stressful, and forces a shutdown. This manifests as mental paralysis.

Working with Your Brain (Step Splitting):

  • Isolate a project and extract the single smallest physical movement required to initiate it (e.g., opening a document).
  • Stack that movement against a sensory micro-dose of novelty (e.g., listening to a specific song).

Botanical Mechanism: Rosemary’s active component, 1,8-cineole, crosses the blood-brain barrier to inhibit acetylcholinesterase, preventing the breakdown of acetylcholine—a critical neurotransmitter linked directly to executive memory and selective task processing speeds.

Week 02

The Kinetic Canvas

🎯 Focus: Mental Offloading & Clarity

If a task is trapped out-of-sight inside an app or notebook, it ceases to exist. We build a physical, sensory-rich wall layout to keep your priorities safely visible without causing eye strain.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Gotu Kola
The Benefit: Improves mental clarity and cognitive capacity under stress.
Protocol: Add 1-2 tsp dried herb to hot water. Cover tightly for 15 minutes to preserve brain-soothing compounds.

🧠 Overcoming Out-of-Sight, Out-of-Mind Amnesia

Why This Module Benefits You: Converting abstract concepts into a highly physical, external roadmap bypasses working-memory weaknesses and provides instant clarity.

Why Overload Happens: Deficits in operational working memory mean an ADHD brain cannot easily manipulate multiple intangible priorities at once. Storing plans inside an app or folder requires permanent mental tracking. Your neural RAM maxes out, creating immediate cognitive clutter and defensive exhaustion.

Working with Your Brain (Step Splitting):

  • Do a complete brain dump onto loose sticky notes—one standalone idea per paper sheet.
  • Physically organize them across three distinct visual spatial tiers on your wall: Now, Next, and Later.

Botanical Mechanism: Gotu Kola (Centella asiatica) safely promotes brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) and balances GABA receptors, protecting complex neural circuits from high oxidative wear and structural stress breakdown.

Week 03

Environmental Scaffolding

🎯 Focus: Defeating Task Friction

Isolate and eliminate the micro-hurdles that trigger executive breakdown. We physically restructure your daily spaces so moving from idea to execution takes zero willpower.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Tulsi (Holy Basil)
The Benefit: An adaptogen that lowers biological stress markers and balances energy response.
Protocol: Decoction method: Lightly simmer 1 tbsp of leaves in a small pot for 5-10 minutes.

🧠 Mitigating Environmental Obstacles & Willpower Drain

Why This Module Benefits You: Designing structural environments reduces reliance on inconsistent internal willpower, making desired behaviors the path of least physical resistance.

Why Overload Happens: Initiating tasks relies on executive function. If the brain detects minor sensory barriers (e.g., a missing cord, a cluttered work surface), the prefrontal cortex burns through its energy reserves assessing those variables. This rapid fuel depletion triggers an immediate task abandonment loop.

Working with Your Brain (Step Splitting):

  • Walk through a target habit to pinpoint the physical step that derails your momentum.
  • Prep your physical environment ahead of time to eliminate that roadblock (e.g., set up your work materials the night before).

Botanical Mechanism: Tulsi functions as an adaptogenic modulator, altering the activity of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis to restrict cortisol overproduction, which helps prevent environmental sensitivity from triggering physical anxiety.

Week 04

Taming the Time Blindness

🎯 Focus: Stress-Free Pacing

Traditional schedules run by numbers on a clock feel like a trap. We transition your calendar to intuitive, visual energy-blocks that naturally flex alongside your brain’s processing speed.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Lemon Balm
The Benefit: Inhibits GABA transaminase, lifting mood and soothing timeline-induced panic.
Protocol: Steep 2 tsp dried leaves in boiling water for 12 mins. Drink when clock anxiety spikes.

🧠 Adapting to Visual Chronometry & Timeline Fears

Why This Module Benefits You: Replacing standard numerical timestamps with tactile, visual blocks removes timeline guilt and lowers systemic transition panic.

Why Overload Happens: Structural differences in the neural pathways connecting the basal ganglia and cerebellum can impair structural time perception. To an ADHD brain, time is often parsed into binary states: "Now" and "Not Now." Forcing this system to rely on numbers creates stress and persistent performance anxiety.

Working with Your Brain (Step Splitting):

  • Stop scheduling tasks by exact minutes; allocate broad theme windows instead (e.g., "Creation Window").
  • Use external physical tracking anchors, like sand timers or color-changing lights, to visualize time moving.

Botanical Mechanism: Lemon Balm contains rosmarinic acid, which targets and down-regulates GABA transaminase. By keeping active GABA accessible in synapses, it provides clear, non-sedating relief from timeline-induced anxiety.

Neuro-Burnout Mitigation

🎯 Focus: Recovery & Energy Preservation

Unmasking your true self takes metabolic energy. Learn how to identify sensory warning indicators early and utilize soothing lifestyle resets to step out of high-cortisol survival states.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Ashwagandha
The Benefit: Calms the HPA axis to dramatically lower circulating cortisol levels.
Protocol: Tonic Latte: Simmer 1/2 tsp root powder in warm milk with cinnamon for 7 mins.

🧠 Defeating Sensory Exhaustion & High-Cortisol Crashes

Why This Module Benefits You: Recognizing early indicators of physical exhaustion lets you intervene before facing full neurodivergent shutdown, helping you manage your baseline energy sustainably.

Why Overload Happens: Poor sensory filtering means an ADHD brain processes ambient sounds, lights, and emotional demands at full volume. Masking these experiences requires continuous metabolic energy. Once your energy reserves are spent, your nervous system triggers a high-cortisol safety shutdown.

Working with Your Brain (Step Splitting):

  • Isolate your distinct sensory triggers (e.g., word-finding difficulties, light sensitivity).
  • Establish non-negotiable micro-recovery windows throughout the day that feature zero sensory input.

Botanical Mechanism: Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) contains specific withanolides that modulate systemic stress responses, supporting adrenal health to stabilize high circulating cortisol levels.
