Meet Your Coach

I worked relentlessly to build a system that works. Because I have ADHD too.

Hello! I’m Tez, an ADHD coach dedicated to helping women break through the exhausting cycles of neurodiverse burnout, step out of their heads, and start living out loud.

I partner with women aged 25–55—both those who are formally diagnosed and those who strongly suspect they have ADHD—who know they are capable of achieving incredible things, but find themselves constantly hitting a invisible wall.

Before designing this curriculum, I spent years cycling through intense waves of hyper-fixation followed by absolute, crushing exhaustion. I bought every calendar planner layout on the market, tried strict corporate time-blocking, and copied standard neurotypical life hacks. None of it stuck because those frameworks were made for brains built completely different from ours.

True transformation occurred when I stopped trying to patch over my executive gaps and started leaning into natural, biological supporting pathways instead. By pairing gentle nervous-system botanical actions with highly interactive physical workspace setups, I discovered how to cultivate consistent drive without forcing myself into masking loops.
