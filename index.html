The 6-Week Strategy & Herbal Curriculum

No overwhelming theories or text-heavy lectures. Work through one bite-sized lifestyle shift each week, paired with an actionable strategy manual and custom herbal medicine guide.

Week 01

Dopamine Mapping

🎯 Focus: Breaking Procrastination Loops

Stop waiting for "motivation" that never comes. Learn how to track your personal neurological spikes and design tiny novelty triggers that make starting boring tasks easy.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Rosemary
The Benefit: Rich in 1,8-cineole, proven to increase memory speed and focus.
Protocol: Steep 1 tbsp fresh leaves covered for 10 mins. Sip during your morning planning hour.
Week 02

The Kinetic Canvas

🎯 Focus: Mental Offloading & Clarity

If a task is trapped out-of-sight inside an app or notebook, it ceases to exist. We build a physical, sensory-rich wall layout to keep your priorities safely visible without causing eye strain.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Gotu Kola
The Benefit: Improves mental clarity and cognitive capacity under stress.
Protocol: Add 1-2 tsp dried herb to hot water. Cover tightly for 15 minutes to preserve brain-soothing compounds.
Week 03

Environmental Scaffolding

🎯 Focus: Defeating Task Friction

Isolate and eliminate the micro-hurdles that trigger executive breakdown. We physically restructure your daily spaces so moving from idea to execution takes zero willpower.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Tulsi (Holy Basil)
The Benefit: An adaptogen that lowers biological stress markers and balances energy response.
Protocol: Decoction method: Lightly simmer 1 tbsp of leaves in a small pot for 5-10 minutes.
Week 04

Taming the Time Blindness

🎯 Focus: Stress-Free Pacing

Traditional schedules run by numbers on a clock feel like a trap. We transition your calendar to intuitive, visual energy-blocks that naturally flex alongside your brain’s processing speed.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Lemon Balm
The Benefit: Inhibits GABA transaminase, lifting mood and soothing timeline-induced panic.
Protocol: Steep 2 tsp dried leaves in boiling water for 12 mins. Drink when clock anxiety spikes.
Week 05

Neuro-Burnout Mitigation

🎯 Focus: Recovery & Energy Preservation

Unmasking your true self takes metabolic energy. Learn how to identify sensory warning indicators early and utilize soothing lifestyle resets to step out of high-cortisol survival states.

🌱 Botanical Ally: Ashwagandha
The Benefit: Calms the HPA axis to dramatically lower circulating cortisol levels.
Protocol: Tonic Latte: Simmer 1/2 tsp root powder in warm milk with cinnamon for 7 mins.

Tie your custom systems together into an automated framework that keeps moving forward even on low-dopamine days. Protect your progress without reverting to hyper-masking loops.
