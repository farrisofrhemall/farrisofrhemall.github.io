From Masking to Empowering: My Story

My passion for this work is deeply personal. For the first 28 years of my life, I knew I felt "different," but without answers, I did what many neurodivergent women do: I masked my struggles, ignored my intuition, and forced myself to power through.

The turning point came when I had my own children. Seeing them face the exact same barriers I had survived was my wake-up call. I wanted answers—not just to set my children up for success, but to heal myself.

With 15 years of experience in education specializing in staff and student development, I began to see a heartbreaking pattern: students labeled as "difficult" or "bad" were actually just profoundly misunderstood. When I met their parents, I recognized the exact same unmet needs. I made it my mission to help entire families work with their superpower rather than fighting against it. Soon, parents began seeking me out for individual guidance, and my coaching practice was born.

Credibility, Credentials & Impact

Over the past 8+ years of individual coaching and 15 years in education, I have dedicated myself to changing the narrative around neurodiversity from one of deficit to one of strength.

✨ Systemic Impact: I was brought into a large, national K–12 school specifically to help neurodiverse students excel. I successfully helped dozens of students bridge academic gaps while giving them the tools to stop masking and fully accept who they are.

🏫 Institutional Training: I train school leadership, corporate staff, and student bodies on how to support the neurodiverse individuals around them without pathologizing them or making them feel like a problem.

🍃 The Holistic Advantage: I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Communications and Education, a formal Certification in ADHD Coaching, and a Certification as an Herbalist. This unique blend allows me to look at ADHD from a truly individualized perspective, breaking down exactly what your unique brain and body need to function at their absolute peak.
